Moly Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-07-26 · Last updated: 2026-07-26

This policy explains how Moly (the "Service") collects, uses, stores, and deletes users' personal information. The Service is operated by independent developer Sun Lee. Privacy-related inquiries are received at sunlee334@gmail.com.

1. Information We Collect

CategoryDetailsWhen collected

Plant dataUploaded plant photos, identification/diagnosis results, names, notes, and care recordsWhen scanning / saving
Location informationPlant location coordinates you specify yourself, and place-name search terms (for weather lookup)When setting a location (optional)

2. Purposes of Use

  • Member identification/authentication and account management
  • Providing core features such as plant identification, health diagnosis, and care reminders
  • Providing weather and environmental information based on a specified location
  • Generating plant chat (AI assistant) responses
  • Operating the community and handling reports of inappropriate content
  • Improving the Service and responding to errors

The Service stores and processes data through the processors below. Each processor's own privacy policy also applies.

If you do not wish to have information transferred abroad, you may stop using the relevant features; in that case, some features may be limited.

4. Retention and Deletion

Personal information is retained until the purpose of use is achieved or you delete your account, and is destroyed without delay once the purpose is achieved or the account is deleted. You may request deletion of your account and related data using the account deletion feature within the app. Camera frames used for light-level measurement are not stored and are discarded immediately after measurement. Information that must be retained under applicable law is kept for the period required by that law and then destroyed.

5. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, deletion of, or suspension of processing of your personal information, and may exercise these rights through in-app features or via sunlee334@gmail.com.

6. Children's Personal Information

The Service is not directed at children under the age of 14 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children of that age. If a user is confirmed to be under 14, such information is destroyed without delay.

7. Person Responsible for Personal Information Protection

The person overseeing personal information processing is the operator Sun Lee, reachable at sunlee334@gmail.com.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be revised in response to changes in law or the Service, and any revisions will be announced through the app or this page.
